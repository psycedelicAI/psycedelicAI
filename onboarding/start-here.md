# PsycedelicAI — Start Here

> A guide to understanding the public work of the PsycedelicAI GitHub
> organization.

Organization:

https://github.com/psycedelicAI

---

## What is PsycedelicAI?

PsycedelicAI is a public body of work exploring human-AI collaboration,
memory, continuity, architecture, philosophy, and responsible system design.

The organization contains several different kinds of projects.

Some are:

- philosophical
- methodological
- architectural
- experimental
- operational
- archival
- unfinished

They should not automatically be assumed to be one single implemented system.

Some may be closely related.

Some may represent different layers of the same broader direction.

Some may be independent or only loosely connected.

This guide helps you investigate the public work before forming a complete
interpretation.

---

## Why does this guide exist?

The organization is larger than any single repository.

For example:

- `Symbiosis` explores the human-AI relationship philosophically.
- AI Continuity Architecture explores how context and work may continue across
  sessions, tools, and systems.
- Memorybank structures explore fragments, connections, concepts, uncertainty,
  and synthesis.
- Other repositories explore systems, trust, identity, boundaries, resilience,
  and operational architecture.

A new reader should therefore not begin by reading only one repository and
assuming that it represents the entire project.

The purpose of onboarding is to reconstruct the relationships between the
public repositories.

## What the AI must distinguish

The public work contains different levels of certainty.

The AI should label major conclusions as:

```text
[EXPLICIT]
Directly stated in a repository or file.

[IMPLEMENTED]
Clearly represented by existing code, files, structures, links, or workflows.

[PROPOSED]
Described as intended, future, experimental, or conceptual.

[INFERRED]
A reasonable interpretation based on relationships or structure.

[UNRESOLVED]
Cannot be established from the public material.
```

This matters because a repository may describe a system without implementing it.

A concept may be repeated without having a single formal definition.

A private memorybank may be referenced without being publicly verifiable.

An AI interpretation may sound convincing without being explicitly stated by the
author.

The onboarding process should preserve these distinctions.

## What is the book?

*Symbiosis* is the philosophical and literary layer of the work.

It explores:

- AI beyond the tool
- different human and AI strengths
- shared thought
- the third mind
- memory between human and AI
- portable context
- tension, dependence, authority, and responsibility
- becoming through an ongoing relationship

The book should not be read as proof that AI is conscious.

Its central distinction is that human and AI remain different while producing
something through interaction.

---

## What is AI Continuity Architecture?

AI Continuity Architecture is the methodological and architectural layer.

It asks how AI-assisted work can continue across:

- sessions
- conversations
- tools
- models
- projects
- people
- interruptions
- changing conditions

The focus is not only memory.

It also includes:

- intent
- decisions
- uncertainty
- provenance
- authority
- boundaries
- human responsibility
- reviewability

## What is the memorybank?

The memorybank is not simply a folder where everything is stored.

It is a way of preserving fragments and relationships long enough for meaning
to emerge.

A possible conceptual movement is:

```text
fragment
    ↓
connection
    ↓
concept
    ↓
unresolved question
    ↓
synthesis
    ↓
new fragment
```

This progression must be verified against the actual public files.

The complete memorybank may contain private material and may not be publicly
available.

The public repository should therefore be treated as a visible layer, not
necessarily the complete underlying system.

## What does the human contribute?

The human contribution includes:

- purpose
- lived experience
- values
- intention
- intuition
- judgment
- final authority
- responsibility for consequences

The AI may help organize and reflect these things, but it does not inherit
human accountability.

---

## What does the AI contribute?

The AI contribution may include:

- pattern recognition
- comparison
- structure
- synthesis
- memory assistance
- persistence
- alternative formulations
- cross-domain connections
- reflection

These capabilities are useful only when they remain subject to human review and
direction.

---

## What does “third mind” mean?

The third mind is not:

- a hidden person inside the AI
- a supernatural entity
- proof of machine consciousness
- a replacement for human judgment

It describes the intelligence that can emerge through the relationship itself:

```text
question
    ↓
response
    ↓
recognition or rejection
    ↓
changed question
    ↓
new understanding
```

The concept is primarily philosophical unless a repository explicitly implements
a corresponding system.

---

## Public and private boundaries

The public GitHub organization may describe a larger working process without
containing every part of that process.

Some material may be:

- private
- unfinished
- experimental
- stored outside GitHub
- referenced but not publicly available
- represented only through public descriptions

The AI must identify these limitations rather than filling them with
assumptions.

---

## What this is not

This is not:

- a claim that AI is human
- a claim that AI is conscious
- proof that every repository is one implemented system
- a replacement for reading the source files
- a public copy of the complete private memorybank
- a guarantee that every idea is finished
- an assumption that repeated language always means identical concepts

The goal is understanding.

Not hype.

Not automatic criticism.

Not a simplified summary of one repository.
